About Kamlesh Sir

Motion to Meditation.

Kamlesh Sir has been guiding students through the ancient practice of yoga for over 20 years in the heart of Jodhpur, Rajasthan.

His teaching is rooted in classical Indian tradition — Hatha, Vinyasa, Pranayama, and deep Meditation — offered with warmth, patience, and devotion to every student who walks through the door.

Whether you are 8 or 80, a complete beginner or an experienced practitioner, SK Yoga Classes is your sanctuary.

Yoga is not what you do — it is what you become.
