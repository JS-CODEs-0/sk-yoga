About Kamlesh Sir

Motion to Meditation.

Kamlesh Sir has been guiding students through the ancient practice of yoga for over 16 years in the heart of Jodhpur, Rajasthan.

His teaching is rooted in classical Indian tradition — Hatha, Vinyasa, Pranayama, and deep Meditation — offered with warmth, patience, and devotion to every student who walks through the door. He holds a Diploma in Yoga, M.A in Yoga, NET and JRF in Yoga, and is a certified Yoga Alliance 500-hour teacher.

Whether you are 8 or 80, a complete beginner or an experienced practitioner, S.K. Yoga Center is your sanctuary.

Yoga is not what you do — it is what you become.
